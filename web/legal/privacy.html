3. Where the data is stored

ATEM Hybrid uses Google services as processors under Art. 28 GDPR:

  • Firebase Authentication — signing in with a Google account.
  • Cloud Firestore — storing training, profile and settings data.

The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

Storage location: eur3 (European Union). This project's Firestore database is pinned to the eur3 multi-region, so training data is stored within the EU.

Access from third countries cannot be entirely ruled out in technical terms, for example during Google support or maintenance. Google bases such transfers on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Local storage on the device

The app also caches data on the device so it stays usable without an internet connection. That copy is removed when the app is uninstalled.

4. Disclosure to third parties

Beyond the processors named in section 3 there is no disclosure. No analytics, tracking or advertising services are embedded. No profiles are built for advertising, and no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

The figures shown in the app — such as the readiness score — are calculated from the user's own training data and have no legal or similarly significant effect.

5. Retention

Data is kept for as long as the account exists. If the account is deleted, the associated training, profile and settings data is deleted with it. Deletion can be triggered in the app or requested informally by email.

7. Closed beta

ATEM Hybrid is in a closed testing phase. Access is limited to explicitly approved accounts. To that end, signing in checks whether the user ID or email address appears on an allowlist. That list holds only an identifier, an email address and an approval flag.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the processing changes, for instance with new features. The version published here is the one that applies; the date above gives its status.
